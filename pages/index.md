[](https://twitter.com/alysonrmartin)Hi there.

I am the co-founder of [Cannabis Wire](https://cannabiswire.com), where I write a daily newsletter focused on cannabis policy, regulation, and research. I am also the co-author of *A New Leaf: The End of Cannabis Prohibition* (The New Press).

More broadly, my work spans academia, journalism, and entrepreneurship. Since 2016, I have taught graduate courses that include reporting, ethics, and the business of media, and I have advised on capstone theses. I an also an [editor](https://journalism.columbia.edu/directory/alyson-r-martin) and [publisher](https://www.nycitynewsservice.com/contact-us/) of exemplary graduate student journalism, and I have guided hundreds of students through the publishing process. 

As a former grant recipient (Brown Institute Magic Grant, City of New York), I'm open to collaboration. I'd love to learn about what you're working on, and areas for collaboration in cannabis, journalism, academia, or all of the above.
